# M4 — contact geometry: gate FAIL

All 840 frames audited; intent interval [260,550), 290 frames. Targets [0,±.17,.14] are exactly on exposed side3/side4 walls (distance < 1e-7 m), below handles. EEF offsets [.05,0,0] match YAML and NPZ. Target G1 FK matches recorded wrists within 8.19e-8 m and 8.30e-8 rad: no evidence of a robot/world coordinate mismatch.

This does not pass the physical-contact gate. Desired-contact EEF errors (left/right): median 41.80/42.61 mm, P95 85.73/86.37 mm, max 96.10/118.22 mm. Native hand/wrist collisions penetrate in 192/231 of 290 frames, maximum primitive-pair depths 37.22/44.99 mm. HDMI boxes penetrate in 141/207 frames, maximum 31.07/57.89 mm. Both also lose geometric contact during parts of desired-contact intent. Thus simply switching to HDMI boxes does not reconcile the reference.

EEF is an internal marker, not a palm surface point. Translating each hand to make EEF equal target at its recorded orientation still creates overlap in every desired-contact frame (both profiles). This is an unconstrained geometric probe, not an IK reachability proof. A zero point-error reward cannot certify nonpenetrating contact. Keep the official target/EEF convention as a semantic reference; a physical surface-contact contract needs explicit acceptance/design before training. Do not change robot reference or increase weights to mask the issue.

CSV files record every target/EEF/wrist pose, surface normal, per-geom-pair signed distance and profile/side aggregate per frame. Negative pair distance means overlap, positive separation; deepest primitive pair is not compound minimum translation depth. No force is inferred and no dynamics stepped. Contact target distances are exact exposed-surface distances; auxiliary point-inside-hand values use min primitive SDF (not exact union exit distance inside overlapping primitives).
