# Candidate decisions before dynamics

Full geometry results are in eef_candidates.*, hand_candidates.*, hand_ablations.csv, support_candidates.*, support_footprints.csv and box_pose_feasibility.csv.

- Official EEF/target: retain as semantic markers. Official hdmi-base.yaml uses eef_contact_exp pos_sigma=.3 m, separate force threshold 10 N; it is not an exact kinematic constraint. The best unconstrained constant offset reduces RMS point error from 48.87/51.71 mm to 28.35/28.33 mm but invents lateral offsets -36.8/+41.9 mm and still cannot change collision overlap. X=80/100 mm makes errors worse. Fitting a wall target barely helps left and moderately helps right. None is a physical repair.
- Native vs HDMI: neither passes. A 5 mm capsule-radius reduction or 20% HDMI thickness reduction trades less penetration for more separation and remains deeply overlapping. These diagnostic shape reductions are rejected for dynamics: no source asset provenance supports them.
- Orientation ablation: freezing wrist orientation relative to box at frame 260 reduces worst right HDMI overlap from 57.89 to 30.94 mm but leaves large overlap and separation. It is not a legal robot trajectory and will not be used dynamically. ±10% box scale similarly fails; official seven-part scale=1 remains.
- Bounded box-pose probe: fixed original robot, 13 diagnostic frames per profile, per-axis translation ±30 mm and rotation-vector components ±5 degrees (norm bounds are consequently larger). Multi-start local search found feasible near-contact poses only in subsets; no full-motion reconciliation. These are geometric probes, not a continuous trajectory or supported equilibrium, and are not applied.
- Support Z=+10 mm closes endpoints but introduces 2.854/5.694 mm overlap, at frames 253/544. +7 mm still penetrates final placement. +4 mm does not penetrate any frame but leaves 6 mm endpoint gaps. The maximum common raise with <=1 mm overlap is about 5.306 mm, while endpoints need >=9.000 mm for <=1 mm gap. No static-height-only correction satisfies both tolerances.
- Selected baseline for physical tests: nominal seven-part box, original COM/inertia, original EEF and targets, original support geometry/poses, native robot feet/inertias. Compare native and HDMI hand-only profiles without preselecting a winner. Include +4 and +10 mm support shifts as explicitly rejected/diagnostic controls to test their physical effect; never alter the original manifest.

Short tests use pure original position PD reference targets, not an existing learned checkpoint. Planned reset windows start at frames 250 (pickup), 400 (carry), 530 (placement/release), 550 (release intent), 839 (terminal settle). Initial penetrating states are reported, not stabilized with a hidden pre-roll. Isolated box-only endpoint drops test whether the 10 mm gap is a reference mismatch that gravity can settle, versus physical support instability. These tests cannot prove an entire successful carry sequence.
